What I work on

My field is applied machine learning and natural language processing. I focus on making language models small enough, fast enough, and reliable enough to ship.

  • Efficient small language models

    Pruning, distillation, and discriminative re-architecture to make language models orders of magnitude faster and smaller while keeping or improving accuracy, so they can serve real-time traffic on commodity hardware.

    • Layer pruning
    • Distillation
    • Latency engineering

  • Intent understanding and routing

    Hybrid intent and entity classifiers that decide whether a request should be answered deterministically or handed to an agent, with confidence-gated routing and self-correcting training data.

    • Intent + slot models
    • LLM-as-judge labeling
    • Synthetic data

  • Agentic AI systems

    Bounded, tool-using agents with explicit policies, evaluation harnesses, and failure analysis, built for operational settings where a wrong action costs real money.

    • Tool routing
    • Guardrails
    • Evaluation

  • Interpretability and evaluation

    Mechanistic studies of how transformer layers hand off entity-tracking state, plus reproducible evaluation methods for voice and text agents. I publish negative results too.

    • Circuit attribution
    • Probing
    • Benchmark design

Current research directions

Work in progress, not yet published: discriminative small language models for joint intent and slot prediction; a cross-architecture study of where entity-tracking state is handed off inside transformers; tool-description effects on multi-turn function calling; and a reporting standard for real-time voice-agent evaluation.

Open source

Public, runnable, and tested, so anyone can check the claims.

  • Practical AI Systems

    30 small, honest AI systems at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels. Each one has a standard-library Python version, tests, a synthetic evaluation, documented limitations, and a zero-dependency web app. Built to take outside contributions.

    • Try all 30 in the browser

  • Voice-agent evaluation corpus

    A verified metadata corpus of 41 sources on real-time voice agents, plus TRG (Timing-Recovery-Grounded), a proposed minimum reporting standard for voice-agent evaluation.

  • Awesome Entity Extraction and Multimodal Extraction

    Curated, LLM-era guides to tools and models for entity, relation, and structured-data extraction from text and images. Every link is checked in CI.

  • tinymorph

    Self-improving training data for small language models through temporal weak-to-strong curation, with no external APIs.

  • GenAI Playlist

    Fundamental generative-AI projects built from scratch, including a GPT-style model in PyTorch, retrieval-augmented generation, and fine-tuning.
